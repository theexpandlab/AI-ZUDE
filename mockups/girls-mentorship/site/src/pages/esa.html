How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. Pick her program

    Choose the camp week, Peer Leader path or One-Day that fits her.

    Write to hello@girlsmentorship.com and tell us you'd like to use ESA funds, plus which program and date.

  3. Get your invoice

    We create an invoice for you and send it back.

  4. Submit in ClassWallet

    Upload the invoice through ClassWallet for reimbursement.

ESA questions

Good questions. Real answers.

Is Girls Mentorship an approved ESA vendor?

Yes. We're an approved Arizona ESA vendor, and families can use ESA funds for camp, Peer Leader enrollment and One-Days.

How do I get an invoice?

Email hello@girlsmentorship.com and let us know which program you're registering for. We'll create an invoice for you to submit through ClassWallet for reimbursement.

Can you help if I get stuck in ClassWallet?

Absolutely. If you need help with the reimbursement process, email us and we'll be happy to help.
