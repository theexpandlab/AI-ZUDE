Power Up Club questions

Good questions. Real answers.

What is this program all about?

A six-week after-school program for girls in grades 4–6 to build confidence, develop social skills, and grow. Using our POWER framework, we guide girls through fun, engaging activities that help them embrace their individuality, navigate challenges with resilience, and build meaningful connections.

What does it cost, and what's included?

$149 per girl for six sessions, one a week after school. Every session is packed with activities that build growth, confidence, and connection in a safe, supportive environment.

How will this help her become a better leader?

Mentorship: guidance from experienced leaders like Jill and Mary. Community: supportive relationships with peers that last beyond the program. Personal development: self-reflection and skill practice that build real life lessons.

Which schools offer it?

The Power Up Club runs on campus at participating schools, and grade levels vary by school. Join the wait list with her school's name and we'll let you know.
