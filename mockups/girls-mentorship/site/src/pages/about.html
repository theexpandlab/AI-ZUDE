How it started

August 2020, and one question.

Back in August 2020, we had a moment of reflection: what would our younger selves have achieved if they'd had the personal growth tools we have now?

Everywhere we looked, we saw young girls grappling with confidence issues, battling negative self-talk, wrestling with poor self-image, and navigating the tricky waters of anxiety and depression. It struck a chord. Weren't we in those same shoes just 20 years ago?

Even with the passage of time, girls were still missing the vital skills to rise above these challenges.

That moment led to a decision. We wanted to be the beacon of change, and extend a helping hand to the young girls who mirrored our past selves. Our mission? To build their social-emotional learning skills and guide them toward the brightest, most confident versions of themselves.

And what an incredible journey it's been, and continues to be. Today that looks like Pursue More Summer Camp, One-Days, the Power Up Club in schools, the Peer Leader program, 1:1 Power Coaching, and the POWER Method we train other women to teach.

Empowering your girl, wherever she's at

What we believe.

At the heart of everything we do is a commitment to empowering youth with the skills they need to thrive. Whether it's confidence, resilience or leadership, our programs are designed for meaningful growth.

We believe in the power of connection: building relationships that support not just girls, but the families, educators and mentors who guide them. Wherever she is on her journey, we offer flexible, tailored support to meet her unique needs.

The POWER Framework

Five pillars behind every program.

Confidence isn't left to chance. We build it through repetition, guidance and experience.

P

Perspective

Slow down her thinking, challenge unhelpful stories, and see situations clearly.

O

Ownership

Take responsibility for how she responds, communicates and shows up.

W

Worthiness

An internal sense of "I'm enough" that doesn't depend on likes or comparison.

E

Empowerment

Speak up, set boundaries, and act even when it's uncomfortable.

R

Reassurance

Regulate emotions, recover from mistakes, and keep trusting herself.
