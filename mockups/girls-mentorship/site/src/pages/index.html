How confidence is actually built

Every program runs on POWER.

Confidence isn't a personality trait. It's the result of how a girl learns to think, respond and trust herself, especially when things feel uncomfortable. Our signature framework gives her a simple, repeatable way to lead herself in real life.

P

Perspective

Slow down her thinking, challenge unhelpful stories, and see situations clearly so emotions don't run the show.

O

Ownership

Take responsibility for how she responds, communicates and shows up. Confidence comes from choices, not approval.

W

Worthiness

Build an internal sense of "I'm enough" that doesn't depend on likes, attention or comparison.

E

Empowerment

Use her voice in small moments and bigger ones: speak up, set boundaries, and act even when it's uncomfortable.

R

Reassurance

Regulate emotions, recover from mistakes, and move forward without losing trust in herself.

Inspired to lead

The heart behind Girls Mentorship.

You want your daughter to thrive: to feel confident in who she is, navigate friendships with ease, and grow into a leader with purpose. But today's world can make that feel like an uphill battle.

We get it because we've been there too. Back in August 2020, we asked ourselves what our younger selves could have done with the tools we have now. Everywhere we looked, girls were grappling with confidence, negative self-talk, and anxiety. Weren't we in those same shoes 20 years ago?

So we built the place we wish we'd had. Since then we've worked with over 100 schools and youth organizations and more than 3,000 girls, with a proven curriculum and a whole lot of fun.
