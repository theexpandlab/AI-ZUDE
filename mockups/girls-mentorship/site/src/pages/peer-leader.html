Raising a high school daughter today

She's not a little kid. She's not an adult yet.

Girls this age are expected to be confident, responsible, emotionally aware, and ready for what's next, often before they've had many chances to practice those skills.

This generation gets labeled distracted, unmotivated, and not ready to lead. But many girls aren't lacking capability. They're lacking opportunities to practice leadership in environments that expect more of them and support them.

You may recognize this in her:

  • She's capable, but hesitant to step forward
  • She waits to be told what to do instead of trusting her judgment
  • She avoids leadership roles because she doesn't feel prepared
  • She has confidence, but struggles to turn it into action

If she doesn't get leadership reps now, she'll be expected to lead later without them.

Peer Leader questions

Good questions. Real answers.

Still wondering? Email info@girlsmentorship.com

How much responsibility will she actually have?

Peer Leaders play a meaningful role, but they are never expected to manage safety decisions or operate independently. They support campers, assist with activities, and help model the culture of camp, with clear expectations and adult leadership present at all times.

What's the difference between Foundations and Advanced?

Foundations is for first-time Peer Leaders learning to lead themselves while supporting campers with guidance. Advanced is for returning Peer Leaders ready to take on more responsibility and mentor others. Both are structured and supported.

Does she need to have attended camp first?

No. If she's in high school and ready to grow through leadership, responsibility, and mentorship, this is a place she belongs.

Can I use ESA funds?

Yes. We're an approved ESA vendor. Email hello@girlsmentorship.com and we'll create an invoice for you to submit through ClassWallet for reimbursement.

What is your refund policy?

Before May 1: full refund, minus a $50 processing fee. May 1 through May 29: 50% refund. After that: no refunds, as her spot has been reserved and camp planning is finalized.

When does registration end?

Registration stays open until spots are full. We've sold out the last four years in a row, so we recommend joining the wait list to hear the moment applications open.
